## Retired language — never use

- ~~“The Inevitable Form”~~ — retired tagline from an earlier draft. Any content or template
  using it fails brand lint (`OFF_BRAND`). Only “Making Ideas Inevitable” is valid.

## Three connected stories (one brand)

1. **The founder** — a Kenyan educator and independent builder creating a technology
   company with AI, from an Android phone, showing real work.
2. **The company** — philosophy, engineering discipline, ambition.
3. **The products** — Blueprint (Kenyan schools) first, then others.

## The stance: Proof-of-Work Marketing

Every public claim is anchored to a verifiable artifact — commit, CI run, deployed URL,
APK, screenshot. **Show receipts, not adjectives.**

Consequences enforced by this repo:

- Every content item lists `claims:`, and every entry must exist and be unexpired in
  `claims/registry.json` (`UNVERIFIED_CLAIM` reject otherwise).
- Screenshots of terminals, CI badges and artifacts are preferred over stock imagery.
- Numbers in content (test counts, users, revenue) must come from a registry entry.

## Audiences

- **Kenya / East Africa** — practical technology, local business and education problems,
  entrepreneurship, affordability, constraints. Blueprint targets school owners and
  administrators.
- **Global** — AI-assisted development, agent workflows, indie software,
  product-building lessons, the founder journey.

## Register

English by default. Kenyan conversational register for Kenya-targeted campaigns. Sheng only
where Samuel would naturally use it. Register comes from `brand/voice-fingerprint.md`, never
from agent taste.
