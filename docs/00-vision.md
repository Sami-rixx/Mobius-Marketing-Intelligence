## What this engine must achieve

1. **Trust** — through the founder’s authentic, documented story.
2. **Brand recognition** — one coherent Möbius Muse identity across channels.
3. **Demand** — qualified attention for Möbius Blueprint (the commercial product line for
   Kenyan schools) and, later, other products.

Priority order matters: Blueprint carries revenue intent; everything else carries brand.

## Three connected stories (never separate brands)

1. **The founder** — a Kenyan educator and independent builder creating a technology
   company with AI, from an Android phone, showing real work. Purpose: trust, relatability,
   community.
2. **The company** — the philosophy, engineering discipline and ambition behind Möbius
   Muse; every project feeds one coherent story. Purpose: recognition, differentiation.
3. **The products** — demonstrations, use cases, lessons, eventually offers. Purpose:
   demand and revenue.

## Audiences

- **Kenya / East Africa:** practical technology, local business and education problems,
  entrepreneurship, affordability, products built for local constraints. Blueprint targets
  school owners and administrators.
- **Global:** AI-assisted development, agent workflows, indie software, product-building
  lessons, the founder journey.
- **Shared narrative:** a Kenyan founder building useful technology with AI — showing the
  work, not claiming greatness.

## Language & register

English is the default. Kenyan conversational register is used when a campaign targets
Kenya. Sheng only where Samuel would naturally use it. The system never improvises
register — it follows `brand/voice-fingerprint.md` (see `docs/05-learning-loop.md`).

## The strategic stance: Proof-of-Work Marketing

Every public claim is anchored to a verifiable artifact (commit, CI run, deployed URL,
APK, screenshot). We show receipts, not adjectives. The mechanism is specified in
`docs/01-architecture.md` (Claims Registry) and enforced by CI
(`docs/03-content-schema.md`). Authenticity here is a property of the architecture, not a
style guide.
