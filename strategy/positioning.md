# Positioning

## One sentence

Möbius Muse builds useful technology in Kenya with AI — in public, with receipts — and the
first product, Möbius Blueprint, serves Kenyan schools.

## The stance

**Proof-of-Work Marketing.** Every public claim is anchored to a verifiable artifact:
commit, CI run, deployed URL, APK, screenshot. We show receipts, not adjectives.

## Three connected stories (never separate brands)

1. **Founder** — a Kenyan educator and independent builder creating a company with AI, from
   an Android phone, showing real work.
2. **Company** — the philosophy, engineering discipline and ambition; every project feeds
   one coherent story.
3. **Products** — Möbius Blueprint for Kenyan schools first, then others.

## Differentiation

| Others say | Möbius Muse shows |
|---|---|
| "revolutionary AI" | a failing test, then the commit that fixed it |
| "10x your workflow" | the actual agent run and what it cost |
| "built for scale" | the 4G phone it was built on |
| "thought leadership" | a bug report, a fix, a receipt |

## What we refuse

- Hype vocabulary and engagement bait.
- Claims without registry evidence.
- Guru framing: Samuel is learning in public, not teaching from a summit.
- Manufacturing urgency or inventing metrics.

## Priority order

Blueprint carries revenue intent; everything else carries brand trust and recognition. When
in doubt, protect trust.
